Most bids that fail were not lost on price. They were lost on something that could have been checked in the first hour. Before you commit, run through these five.

## 1. Confirm you are eligible
Read the notice for required registrations, tax documents, categories and experience. Requirements vary by buyer, so do not assume. If one is missing, find out now whether you can get it before the deadline.

## 2. Read the evaluation criteria
The buyer usually says how bids are scored. Note what carries the most weight and write your response around it, not around what you find easiest to say.

## 3. Be honest about capacity
Can you deliver this alongside your current work? A strong bid you cannot resource is a risk to your reputation as well as your margin.

## 4. Price the whole scope
Include logistics, compliance costs, renewals, insurance and the time your team will spend on reporting. Missing costs are what turn a won contract into a loss.

## 5. Check your paper trail
Confirm that every document is current, signed where required and consistent across the bid. Expired or mismatched documents are a common reason for disqualification.

If any of the five is unclear, resolve it before you write a word of the response.
